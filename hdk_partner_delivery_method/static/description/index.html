Hamdek Partner Delivery Method

When you choose a delivery method on a customer’s contact, their web checkout can show only that option—so each buyer sees the shipping choice you allow for them.

What you get

Uses the same delivery field you already have on the customer. Set it once; the shop follows it at checkout. Leave it empty and customers keep the full list of available shipping methods, like before.

Per customer

Decide which shipping method each contact may use for online orders.

No extra fields

Works with the standard delivery method on the contact form.

Fits your process

Usual rules for carriers, products, and addresses still apply first.

Key Features

Checkout list

The delivery step only shows methods you allow for that customer when a method is set on the contact.

Optional control

If the contact field is left blank, every suitable carrier for the order remains available.

Multi-company

Respects company-specific values on the customer where Odoo stores them.

How to Get Started

  1. Install Hamdek Partner Delivery Method.
  2. On a customer, set Delivery Method to the allowed shipping option.
  3. Open your online shop with that customer and complete the flow to the delivery step.

Workflow

Set the allowed shipping option on the customer, then see how the online shop responds when it matches—or when no option is available yet.

1

Choose the customer’s delivery method

In the back end, open the contact and select the shipping method they are allowed to use.

2

The shop shows that shipping option

On the website, the delivery step lists the method that matches what you allowed for this customer.

3

When nothing fits yet

If the carrier cannot be used for this order or setup still needs adjustment, the buyer may not see a shipping choice until things align.
