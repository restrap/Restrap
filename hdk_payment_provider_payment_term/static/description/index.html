Hamdek Payment Provider Payment Terms

Choose which payment methods each customer sees in your web shop based on the payment terms you already use for them in accounting—so checkout stays aligned with how you do business.

What it does for you

You decide which payment method goes with which payment term (for example “pay now” for one group and “invoice me later” for another). Customers only see the options that match their terms at checkout.

Set up once

For each way you get paid (card, bank, etc.), say which payment terms it applies to.

Match the customer

Each customer keeps their usual payment terms on their contact. The shop uses that to know what to offer.

A cleaner checkout

Shoppers are not shown payment methods that do not fit their terms—less confusion, fewer mistakes.

Key Features

Tied to payment terms

Link each online payment option to the payment terms it should follow—per customer situation.

Smart listing

Only the payment methods that fit the current order and customer are offered at pay time.

Safer checkout

Customers cannot force a payment method that does not match their terms through the checkout flow.

Works with Odoo

Built for the standard online shop checkout. Your usual delivery and shipping rules still apply as in Odoo.

How to Get Started

  1. Install Hamdek Payment Provider Payment Terms on your database.
  2. Open your payment methods (payment providers) and, for each one, choose which payment terms it belongs to.
  3. On each customer, set their payment terms the same way you already do for invoicing.
  4. Place a test order in your online shop and go to the payment step to confirm the right options appear.

Use Cases

Different rules for different buyers

Let “pay by invoice” appear only for customers you invoice on account, and card payment for everyone who pays up front—automatically on the website.

Same story as your invoices

What you agree in payment terms on the customer matches what they can select when they pay online.

Workflow

From back-office setup to what the buyer sees when they pay—step by step.

1

Tell each payment method who may use it

In your payment provider settings, choose the payment term that matches this method—for example which customers paying on account may use it.

2

Keep customer payment terms up to date

On the customer contact, set how they pay you (immediate, end of month, etc.) so the shop knows which group they belong to.

3

Customer sees the right options

At checkout, when their terms match what you configured, they see the payment methods they are allowed to use.

4

When nothing matches, nothing extra is offered

If no payment method fits that customer’s terms until you adjust settings, they will not see mismatched choices—fix the setup and try again.
