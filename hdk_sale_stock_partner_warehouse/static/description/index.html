Hamdek Sale Stock Partner Warehouse

Store a default warehouse on each customer. New draft sales orders then open with that warehouse—unless you leave the field empty and Odoo keeps your usual company default.

What you get

A single Sale warehouse field on the contact (per company). Optionally, your company can prefer the delivery address partner’s warehouse first, then fall back to the invoice customer.

Per customer

Route fulfilment from the warehouse that matches each buyer or contract.

Shipping option

Turn on company settings to read the ship-to partner’s warehouse when it helps.

Draft orders

Defaults apply while the quotation is draft; standard Odoo rules stay in charge after confirmation.

Key Features

Partner warehouse

Company-dependent field on the contact to drive the default warehouse on new orders.

Optional ship-to rule

Settings toggle to prioritise the delivery address partner’s warehouse when both partners differ.

Fits sale_stock

Extends standard sale and stock integration—no duplicate warehouse master data.

How to Get Started

  1. Install Hamdek Sale Stock Partner Warehouse (requires Sale Stock).
  2. On a customer, set Sale warehouse for each company that should use a dedicated default.
  3. Under Sales → Configuration → Settings, enable Prioritize Shipping Partner in Sale Warehouse Proposal.
  4. Create a quotation and confirm the warehouse field matches your expectation while the order is draft.
